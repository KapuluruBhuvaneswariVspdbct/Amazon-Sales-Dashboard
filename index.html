📊 Amazon Sales Insights Dashboard

🔍 Key Business Insights

  • Top Categories: Electronics and Home & Kitchen are the most profitable categories — recommending increased inventory and ad spend here.
  • Regional Performance: Cities like New York and Los Angeles drive most revenue. Focused marketing in these areas can further improve ROI.
  • Seasonality: Q2 and Q4 show strong sales peaks, indicating opportunities to run targeted promotions and sales events in these periods.
  • Best-selling Products: Smart devices and kitchen appliances consistently lead in demand — ideal for bundling and discount campaigns.
  • Payment Analysis: High cancellation rates linked to Cash on Delivery (COD) — shift toward prepaid discounts to reduce loss.
  • Customer Segmentation: RFM analysis identifies high-value repeat buyers — suggest deploying loyalty programs and retention strategies.
  • Churn Risk: A noticeable segment of users show declining order patterns — recommend re-engagement emails and offers.
